Frequently Asked Questions

How does the 72-hour custody window work? +

Your custody begins the moment our courier delivers the garment capsule to your doorstep or hotel. You retain custody for seventy-two continuous hours, across celebrations, sangeets, and galas. Simply place the unwashed piece back into our return satchel for courier pickup before the window concludes.

What happens if someone spills food or drink on the silk or lehenga? +

Every celebration pass includes complimentary accidental celebration spill coverage! Never attempt to rub or wash raw silk or georgette domestically. Our medical-grade ozone and specialized silk cleansing chambers treat standard celebration blemishes without any extra charge to you.

How are ethnic lehengas, sarees, and corsets sanitized? +

We use 100% medical-grade ozone gas sterilization chambers. Ozone gas destroys 99.9% of bacteria, viruses, and odors without any water immersion or abrasive friction, preserving the original embroidery, zardozi gold threads, and pure silk textures.

When and how do wardrobe lenders receive their payouts? +

Lenders receive a flat 70% direct dividend of every rental fee, transferred automatically to their registered bank/UPI address every Monday morning. You can track your garment's rental history in real-time through your vault dashboard.
