Frequently Asked Questions

How does the 72-hour rental custody window work? +

Your custody begins the moment our courier delivers the garment capsule to your hostel gate, dorm, or doorstep. You retain custody for seventy-two continuous hours, across fests, concerts, and weekend bashes. Simply place the unwashed piece back into our return satchel for courier pickup before the window concludes.

What happens if someone spills a drink during a party or fest? +

Every pass includes complimentary accidental party spill coverage! Never attempt to rub or wash delicate fabrics in a hostel sink. Our medical-grade ozone and specialized cleansing chambers treat standard party blemishes without any extra charge to you.

How are corsets, co-ords, and dresses sanitized? +

We use 100% medical-grade ozone gas sterilization chambers. Ozone gas destroys 99.9% of bacteria, viruses, and odors without any harsh water immersion or abrasive friction, preserving the fabrics, boning, and delicate lace in pristine condition.

When and how do college closet lenders receive their payouts? +

Lenders receive a flat 70% direct dividend of every rental fee, transferred automatically to their registered UPI (GPay/PhonePe/Paytm) every Monday morning. You can track your garment's rental history in real-time through your vault dashboard.
